# Veritas SafeChange — One Page

**Veritas SafeChange** is a **Veritas Aegis** product from **Samantha Revita** and **Terry Snyder**.

## Core promise
A risky production change either lands safely, or it fails closed with proof.

## What the product does
SafeChange places a governed boundary around a state change or deployment path.  
A change is only allowed to become real when it can be applied, verified, and, if needed, reversed cleanly.

## Human-facing outcomes
- **SAFE**
- **REVERTED**
- **BLOCKED**

## What teams get
- provable rollback
- receipt-backed records
- deterministic audit verification
- local and CI-friendly operation
- a simple yes/no control surface for risky change

## Best fit
- risky deploys
- state changes
- upgrade paths
- validation-heavy delivery flows
- teams that need proof, not assumptions
