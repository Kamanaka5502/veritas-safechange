# Veritas Aegis Commercial Terms

This system is licensed, not sold.

## Product position
Veritas SafeChange is a governed execution-boundary system.
It controls whether a change is allowed to become real, whether rollback is available,
and whether the resulting receipt trail remains verifiable and replayable.

## Allowed uses
- Confidential evaluation
- Internal proof-of-concept under agreement
- Demonstration under authorized review

## Not allowed
- Production deployment without a commercial license
- Redistribution or sublicensing
- Reverse engineering or derivative implementation
- Rebuilding equivalent enforcement, rollback, receipt, or replay logic
- Embedding into another commercial product without written agreement

## Commercial principle
The value of Veritas Aegis is in the execution boundary itself:
- controlled commit
- invariant enforcement
- rollback discipline
- receipt generation
- replay verification

## Deployment rights
Production rights require:
- a written commercial agreement
- defined deployment scope
- agreed execution boundary
- approved licensing terms
